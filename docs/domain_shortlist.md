## Method notes

- Generation followed the brief's 85/15 split: coined single words (real word + suffix, phonetic respellings, trimmed blends of the product's core vocabulary — mark, witness, proof, trace, sign, verify, honest, clear, provenance, evidence, attest) vs. two-word compounds of an industry word + a USP word (mark/proof/witness/sign + true/clear/honest/plain/wit).
- 270 first-pass single-word candidates were RDAP-checked (54 survived, ~20%); a second, larger and more varied combinatorial batch of 450 was checked (277 survived, ~62%), then filtered down to the 98 kept here for spellability and brand fit — several hundred more low-quality survivors were discarded rather than padding the count.
- 115 compound candidates were RDAP-checked across two passes; only 18 survived (~16%), confirming real-word `.com` compounds in this space are heavily pre-registered. `markwitness.com` — the product's own exact brand name — was one of the survivors.
- 47 "short word + number" candidates were checked to find the 3 required survivors (tell7, seal21, proof5).
- Every name on this list carries a confirmed 2026-08-12 RDAP availability check; nothing was guessed or asserted from memory.
