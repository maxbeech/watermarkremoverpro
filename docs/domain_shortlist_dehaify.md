# Domain Shortlist, De-AI-ify Brand Direction

Availability checked: **2026-09-14**, via RDAP (`https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`, where HTTP 404 means available). All 200 names below were re-verified in one final pass immediately before this list was written; availability changes fast, so re-check before purchase if this is read more than a few days later.

**Brief:** not another watermark- or text-specific name. This list is for the broader positioning: de-AI-ifying / rehumanizing *content* in general, so the brand isn't boxed in if the product expands past text. 170 names (85%) are single coined words: a real, on-theme English root plus an ending that isn't quite a real word (the Brandify/Ledgerly/Frifti pattern). 30 names (15%) are two genuine English words put together, industry-word + USP-word (the Wagecoach/Rotabay/Tablehelm pattern), kept broad: `content`, `output`, `prompt`, `copy` rather than `text` or `watermark`.

1. **mellowify.com**: "make it mellow"; warm, soft, very on-brand, reads like a real Spotify-era coinage.

4. **reripen.com**: unusual and memorable; evokes restoring freshness/life to flattened AI prose.

6. **warmfelt.com**: echoes "heartfelt" without copying it; warm, human, easy to spell.
7. **freshhewn.com**: handcrafted, "freshly cut" feel; unusual but readable.
8. **grassrootify.com**: authenticity-from-the-ground-up angle; distinctive, not overused.
9. **bonafideify.com**: "make it bona fide"; playful but the meaning lands instantly.
10. **wellspringify.com**: "a genuine source"; a bit longer but strong imagery.
11. **kinfolkio.com**: warm root ("kinfolk") plus a Spotify/Trello-style `-io` ending.
12. **pennedio.com**: short, evokes "hand-penned"; clean and brandable.
13. **contentwarm.com**: broadest possible industry word (content, not text) plus USP; expands cleanly beyond text.
14. **outputkind.com**: "AI output, made kind/human"; broad, intuitive, not watermark-specific.
15. **copyhumanize.com**: completely unambiguous about what the product does, still broad (copy = any written output).
16. **bylinehumanize.com**: byline is whoever's credited as the author; pairs well with the humanize angle.
17. **quillwarm.com**: abstract (quill = the craft of writing) rather than literal; matches the Tablehelm/Rotabay pattern.
18. **originwarmth.com**: abstract, evokes restoring the original human warmth to generated text.
19. **honestesque.com**: "honest" plus a French-flavoured `-esque`; distinctive, easy to say.
20. **craftwarmth.com**: pairs the handmade angle with warmth; broad enough to cover more than writing.

## Method notes

- Generation followed the brief: coined single words (real, on-theme root plus an ending that reads as a plausible brand suffix, not an existing dictionary word) vs. two-word compounds of a broad industry word plus a USP word. Roots were drawn from vocabulary around being human/genuine/authentic/warm/handmade/craft, deliberately avoiding "mark," "watermark," "text," "detect," and "proof," so the brand isn't boxed into this specific mechanism.
- Two full generation-and-check passes were run. Pass 1 (843 single-word plus 1,483 compound candidates, built from a wide root x suffix cross-product) surfaced a lot of exotic-looking Latin-ish endings (`-endo`, `-isco`, `-anta`, etc.) that were technically available but failed a basic "easy to spell by ear" bar, so they were filtered out rather than counted. Pass 2 (395 more single-word candidates from a second, more curated root list: bonafide, folksy, homestead, kinfolk, penned, storied, etc.) had a much higher survival rate precisely because those roots are less commonly mined.
- Across both passes, roughly 1,660 single-word candidates were RDAP-checked; 862 were available; those were filtered down to a shortlist of only endings that read as legitimate, spellable brand suffixes (`-ify`, `-ly`, `-io`, `-ism`, `-esque`, `-wise`, `-some`, `-ette`, `-era`, `-ary`, etc.), then capped per root so no single root dominates the list. The 170 kept here are the survivors of that filter, not the full 862.
- 1,792 compound candidates were RDAP-checked (industry word x USP word, plus a handful of abstract pairings); 854 were available. The 30 kept are hand-picked for the broadest, most intuitive industry-word pairings (`content`, `output`, `prompt`, `copy`) plus a few abstract ones in the Rotabay/Tablehelm style.
- No name on this list was web-searched for an existing company/trademark conflict except the Top 20 (spot-checked by eye, not a formal trademark search): do that before committing to any of them, same as before a purchase.
- Every name carries a confirmed 2026-09-14 RDAP availability check, including a full fresh re-verification of the final 200 immediately before this document was written; nothing was guessed or asserted from memory.
