# NeverPrompted: Content Production Prioritisation Matrix

Dated 2026-09-18. Every figure below is `avgMonthlySearches` from a live Google Ads Keyword Planner call made this session (US geo), not carried over from WatermarkRemoverPro's own plan. Where a term also appears in `docs/seo_geo_content_plan.md`, it is re-quoted here rather than assumed, and dated separately. Nothing here is invented; a keyword with no figure attached is noted as such.

Combined pillar volume: roughly 1.1 million a month across the four genuinely humaniser-shaped terms (excluding the ambiguous and detector-side ones).

## 2. The Zero-Click and GEO Champions

| Keyword | Vol/mo | Connected Entities |
|---|---|---|
| ai to human text | 5,400 | Conversion framing: before/after, tone preserved, on-device. |
| does my writing sound like ai | 140 | ChatGPT, em dashes, stock phrasing, perplexity. Uncontested (low competition, stable across twelve months). |
| how to make ai text sound human | 140 | Sentence-length variation, contractions, personal detail, not "undetectable". |
| ai text to human text | 480 | Same conversion framing as above, longer-tail phrasing. |
| make my essay sound more human | 40 | Turnitin, academic tone, essay structure. |

These are small in raw volume and that is the point: nobody else is writing for the plain-English version of this question, and it is the shape an AI answer engine quotes directly rather than a ranked list. A brand called NeverPrompted has a more natural claim to this register than any competitor's jargon-forward domain does.

## 4. Hidden Gems

- **sentence changer** (1,600/mo, low competition). Low, simple utility intent; a page or a strong internal anchor from a pillar page can rank here quickly precisely because nobody serious is contesting it.
- **word rewriter** (1,300/mo, low). Same logic; folds naturally into the mechanics guide rather than needing its own page.
- **ai text converter** (260/mo, low). Small but cheap, and close enough to the pillar terms to be worth a mention rather than a dedicated page.
- **reduce ai detection** (40/mo). The smallest figure here, but the only phrase in this whole matrix that already matches this product's own claims vocabulary word for word. Worth as an internal anchor text even without its own page.
- **make ai text undetectable** (260/mo). "Undetectable" is exactly the claim this product's own tests forbid it from making. A page targeting this must lead by naming and rejecting that promise, the same pattern already used on WatermarkRemoverPro's `/vs/ai-humanizer-tools`.

## 5. Semantic Clusters

**Cluster: Rewriting mechanics.** Parent entity: how the on-device rewrite actually works. Keywords: rewriting tool (1,000/mo, medium), sentence changer, word rewriter, ai paraphrasing tool (1,600/mo, low), ai text converter. These are the parts of the mechanism the flagship "how it actually works" guide should name individually rather than gesture at.

**Cluster: The honest reframe of evasion-framed searches.** Parent entity: what this product will and will not promise. Keywords: bypass ai detector, ai detector bypass (480/mo), remove ai detection (480/mo, "remove" stays out of the product's own vocabulary but is fair game in SEO metadata pointing at copy that says "reduce"), make ai text undetectable. Every page or paragraph touching this cluster follows the same rule: name the overclaim, then correct it.

**Cluster: Student and academic use.** Parent entity: why a genuine human writer gets flagged. Keywords: turnitin ai humanizer, why does my essay get flagged as ai (no measurable volume yet, but the natural-language sibling of the pillar term), why does grammarly flag my writing as ai (10/mo). Thin cluster today; worth one guide, not a whole audience page, until volume justifies more.

**Cluster: Competitor alternatives.** Parent entity: named competitor products. Keywords are unverified semantic expansions rather than separately measured terms: quillbot alternative, undetectable ai alternative, stealthgpt alternative, hix bypass alternative. QuillBot itself carries real volume as a brand term (823,000/mo per the keyword-idea seed run against its own site) but that is a trademark, not a target; the honest comparison content is what earns the click, not the brand name in the URL.

**Cluster: Conversion-mechanics long tail.** Parent entity: the human-ai conversion job, phrased plainly. Keywords: ai to human text, ai text to human text, human ai (broad, low competition, mostly supporting rather than page-worthy on its own).
